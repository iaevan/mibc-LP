# MIBC Recruitment

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js, requested by the user. Static export hosted through Cloudflare Workers, using Wrangler as requested in the follow-up. No backend is required.

## Users

North South University students exploring the Marketing and International Business Club and deciding whether to join.

## Product Purpose

Five complete and materially different landing-page concepts for MIBC recruitment, available as five routes in one project. Each page introduces the club, gives a compelling reason to participate, and ends in a recruitment action.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Five distinct responsive page layouts, with smooth, restrained animation and strong loading performance.
- Every final application button links to https://forms.google.com until a real form exists.
- Original recruitment copy is authorized by the user.
- The four researched MIBC events are MarXcellence, Innovade, Amelior, and Intra-Ignite; sources are in `docs/event-sources.md`. Recruitment dates, eligibility rules, and final application details remain unsupplied. Do not invent operational promises or numeric club claims.
- Recruitment follows Register, Book an appointment, and Check your mail. Only registration has an action link; booking and mail instructions are explanatory.
- Working concept title: Beyond the Ordinary. This is authored proposal copy, not an official event name.

## Brand Commitments

MIBC means Marketing and International Business Club of North South University. The supplied visual reference uses a deep burgundy/crimson field with fine geometric linework and existing club/university marks. Apple Design and Impeccable were explicitly requested for the implementation.

## Evidence on Hand

The supplied recruitment-theme image. No member photography, testimonials, statistics, recruitment dates, or official recruitment campaign title were supplied. Use abstract artwork and original concept copy, without fabricated endorsements or sponsors.

## Product Principles

- Make membership feel creative, ambitious, and welcoming.
- Give every concept a clearly different composition, not just a new color treatment.
- Keep application actions obvious and accessible.
- Prefer light local assets, native scrolling, and animation that respects reduced motion.
